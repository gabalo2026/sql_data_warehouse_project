# DWH Analytics Requirements Examples

## Context
Simple Data Warehouse structure:
- **Dimensions**: Products, Customers
- **Fact Table**: Sales (with measures: sales_amount, quantity, price)

---

## Customer Behavior
Understanding *who your customers are* and *how they're buying*:

- Which customers spend the most money (highest lifetime value)?
- How often are customers making purchases?
- Are customers buying more or less frequently over time?
- What's the average spend per customer per order?
- Which products does each customer prefer?
- Are there repeat customers vs one-time buyers?
- Can you segment customers by spending patterns (high-value, medium, low)?

---

## Product Performance
Understanding *which products are selling well* and *how they're performing*:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which products sell the highest volumes (quantity)?
- What's the profit margin or average price per product?
- Are certain products trending upward or declining in sales?
- Which products are your worst performers?
- How does each product perform compared to others?
- Do certain products have seasonal demand patterns?
- What types of customers are buying each product?

---

## Sales Trends
Understanding *how sales are changing over time*:

- Is overall revenue increasing, decreasing, or flat?
- How do sales vary by time period (day/week/month)?
- Are there predictable seasonal patterns in sales?
- What were your best and worst performing periods?
- How does this year compare to last year?
- Is growth driven by selling more units or higher prices?
- Are there sudden spikes or drops in sales you should investigate?

---

## Summary
**Customers** = *who*, **Products** = *what*, **Trends** = *when/how much*
